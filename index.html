Low-Cost Monocular 3D Reconstruction and Quantitative Measurement of Foot Soft Tissue
Supervisor: Prof. Zhenan Sun, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences · May 2025 — May 2026

Replaced expensive 3D scanners with an ordinary monocular camera: independently designed the complete pipeline — field video acquisition, SfM reconstruction with COLMAP, DBSCAN point-cloud denoising, and an ID-card reference-object calibration scheme that resolves scale ambiguity. Research · UAB, USA
GenCheck: A LoRA-Adapted Multimodal Large Language Model for Check Analysis
Supervisor: Prof. Chengcui Zhang, University of Alabama at Birmingham · Jan 2024 — Mar 2024

A two-stage framework for financial anti-fraud: Segment Anything separates overlapping checks, a coarse-to-fine pipeline rectifies rotation and perspective distortion, and YOLOv8 models handle classification and element localization; a LoRA-finetuned LLaVA-NeXT then extracts handwritten date, amount, and payee via visual question answering — clearly outperforming traditional OCR and zero-shot MLLM baselines. I built the data pipeline: preprocessing, annotation guidelines, and the multi-task dataset of 8,689 real check images.
